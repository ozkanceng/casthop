CastHop is a free, open-source screen mirroring app that casts your iPhone or iPad screen wirelessly to Pardus ETAP smart boards, Android TV, Windows and Mac computers. Built for teachers: run your lesson from anywhere in the classroom with no cables, no account and no internet connection.

SMART BOARD SCREEN MIRRORING IN TWO STEPS
1. Install the CastHop receiver on the board (a one-click setup guide is at ozkanceng.github.io/casthop).
2. On your iPhone or iPad, tap "Connect to a screen" and scan the QR code shown on the board. Start mirroring. Done.

CastHop remembers every screen you have connected to, so next lesson you reconnect with a single tap.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Show your iPhone or iPad screen and app audio on the smart board: PDFs, slides, videos, digital notebooks and drawing apps.
• Mirror the camera: send a book, notebook or worksheet to the board and use the device as a document camera.
• Hold the device in landscape or portrait; the picture follows the rotation without interruption.
• If the network slows down, picture quality adjusts automatically so delay does not build up.
• Can't scan the QR code? Connect with the 6-digit code shown on the screen.

WHERE YOU CAN MIRROR
• Pardus ETAP 23 smart boards
• Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV
• Windows 10 and later computers
• macOS 14 and later Macs
All receiver apps are free and available at ozkanceng.github.io/casthop.

THE ANDROID APP IS LIVE TOO
CastHop for Android tablets and phones is on Google Play: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sorumatik.casthop

NO CABLES, NO ACCOUNT, NO ADS
• The device and the screen must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Mirroring stays on your local network; your screen is never sent over the internet.
• Connections are encrypted with TLS 1.3. Each screen is paired by QR code or a single-use PIN, so an unknown device cannot cast without permission.
• No sign-up, no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

OPEN SOURCE
The source code is published under the Apache-2.0 license at github.com/ozkanceng/casthop. Visit the page to report issues or contribute.

PERMISSIONS
• Screen recording (broadcast): only to mirror the screen when you start it.
• Camera: to scan the QR code and for optional camera mirroring.
• Local network: to find and connect to the screen on the same network.

GOOD TO KNOW
• DRM-protected content (some streaming services) cannot be mirrored; this is an iOS restriction.
• Requires iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
